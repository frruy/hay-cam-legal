The App is designed to work on your device with minimal data collection. We may collect or process the following:

3. Permissions the App Requests

  • Camera — required to capture photos with the retro film looks the App provides.

You can grant, change, or revoke these permissions at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security on your device.
