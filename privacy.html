4. App-Specific Permissions

The main permissions used by Hay Cam are described below.

4.1 NSCameraUsageDescription

The permission allows the application access to the device camera so you are able to capture photos and apply retro film-style effects, or other related features. The camera is accessed only when you actively use camera-related features within the App.

4.2 NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription

The permission allows the application to save your created captures, edited photos, or exported images to the device’s Photos Library when you actively choose to save them. This permission is used only for saving content generated or selected by you and does not allow the App to access or read your existing photo library.

4.3 NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription

The permission allows the application to read photos from the device’s Photos Library solely to display your captured roll within the in-app experience in the gallery and review screens within the App. This permission is used only for photo viewing and organization features initiated by you and does not access photos without your interaction.

These permissions are essential for the app to function properly and are strictly used in accordance with App Store regulations to ensure a secure, efficient, and user-respecting operation. The app prioritizes user rights and privacy, implementing these permissions to enhance user experience without compromising privacy and security.
